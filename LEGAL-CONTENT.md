# WeserCode legal content

The current legal pages are written around the technical behavior of this project and the verified business contact data supplied for WeserCode.

Before production publication, verify the legal business identity (legal name/form, authorized representative, register details, VAT ID if applicable), the actual hosting provider/log retention, the email delivery provider, and the final Google Analytics/Google Maps configuration. The site intentionally does not invent those missing facts.

The legal copy references the GDPR, DDG and TDDDG requirements at a high level and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before public launch.
